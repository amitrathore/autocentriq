Outcome Machines

Built for how automotive retail actually runs.

The digital twin speaks VINs, deals, repair orders, lenders, and rooftops. Each Outcome Machine turns that shared context into a focused operating loop.

01 / Application

Inventory & appraisal

Every unit acquired, priced, positioned, and turned on evidence.

  • Appraisal guidanceValue each trade against real local retail odds.
  • Aged-unit repricingHold, cut, transfer, or wholesale before gross disappears.
  • Acquisition sourcingBuy the units each market is most likely to retail.

02 / Application

New vehicle & OEM

Allocation, stocking mix, and incentives decided with local demand.

  • Stocking mixOrder to rooftop demand, not last year's average.
  • Incentive allocationPlace support where it is most likely to move metal.
  • Days-supply balancingTrade units between stores before they age.

03 / Application

F&I & lending

Faster, fairer, and fully documented finance decisions.

  • Lender routingMatch each deal to the best-fit approval path.
  • Product fitPrioritize relevant offers for the customer and deal.
  • Decision recordKeep rationale, inputs, and approvals traceable.

04 / Application

Fixed ops & service

Fill bays, protect effective labor rate, and recover deferred demand.

  • Bay & tech capacitySchedule against real technician throughput.
  • Declined-service recoveryRe-engage customers when capacity can serve them.
  • Parts demandAlign stocking with upcoming repair-order needs.

05 / Application

Marketing & leads

Spend and follow-up mapped to profitable retail outcomes.

  • Profit attributionConnect channels to sold units and gross contribution.
  • Lead prioritizationWork the opportunities most likely to close.
  • Follow-up executionKeep valuable demand from going cold.

06 / Application

Dealer group operations

One operating picture and one decision language across every rooftop.

  • Rooftop benchmarkingCompare stores using the same operating definitions.
  • Inventory movementPosition units where they are most likely to sell.
  • Forecast & prioritiesAlign teams around the gaps that change the outcome.

Governed from the start

Every decision needs a clear line of accountability.

Each implementation defines what AutocentrIQ may recommend, what requires approval, and what can run inside agreed limits.

G—01

Control

Roles, permissions, thresholds, budgets, channel rules, and human override.

G—02

Explain

Decision rationale, source context, action history, and outcome attribution.

G—03

Approve

Human review for sensitive or high-impact decisions before execution.

G—04

Audit

A traceable record from operating signal through action and measured result.
